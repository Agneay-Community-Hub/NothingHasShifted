# NOTHING HAS SHIFTED

*A short first-person horror game about an Indian hostel corridor that keeps repeating.*
Made for **GitHub Game Off 2026** (theme: **SHIFT**).

> C-Block Gents Hostel. 2:47 AM. The monsoon has not stopped for three days.
> You only stepped out to fill your water bottle — but the corridor outside your room keeps repeating.

## How to play
- Walk the 8th-floor corridor. Learn what it normally looks like.
- If **anything has shifted**, turn back and take the stairs **behind you**.
- If **nothing has shifted**, keep walking and take the stairs at the **far end**.
- Get it right and you go down a floor. Get it wrong and you're back on the 8th floor.
- Reach the **Ground Floor** to escape.

**Controls:** WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · Esc pause. Headphones recommended.

## Theme: SHIFT
Every floor might hide one of 25 anomalies — a fan spinning backwards, chappals that turn to face you, a corridor tilted by three degrees, rain frozen in mid-air, a white saree on the clothes line, a door number that has shifted, a plastic chair that moves when you look away…

## Built with
- Unity 6 (6000.3) + URP, driven from the Unity CLI / editor automation
- Everything in the scene is generated by `Assets/Editor/SceneBuilder*.cs` (menu: **Game ▸ Build Scene**)
- Custom props (chappals, bucket & mug, water cooler, cricket bat, doors, grille, marigold toran, ghost) modelled procedurally in Blender — see `Blender/build_props.py`
- All sound effects and ambience synthesised in code — `Assets/Editor/ProcAudio.cs`
- Procedural textures (rangoli, kolam, cloth patterns, notices) — `Assets/Editor/ProcTex.cs`
